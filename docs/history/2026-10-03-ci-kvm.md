## Test VMs under KVM in CI

`tools/novarun.py` (and so `tools/selftest.py`) now passes `-accel kvm` when
`/dev/kvm` is readable and writable and `-accel tcg` otherwise;
`NOVARUN_ACCEL=tcg|kvm` forces one.  The CPU model and the SMP counts are
unchanged.  GitHub's Linux runners have `/dev/kvm`; a udev rule step opens it
to the runner user.

**Graphics job: on KVM.**  The `d2dtest`, Mesa/DXVK, `gltest` and `d3dtest`
step went from 193 s (TCG, main at 5bf5b75) to 111 s and passed.

**Core boot-test and nightly corpus: still TCG.**  Under KVM the core
suite's boot died with a #GP in `uacpi_gas_read_mapped` (called from
`uacpi_setup_gpe_for_wake` by the ACPI thread's `add_wake`, with a
non-canonical pointer in RDI) while the other threads were starting, and in
the network suite `winsock IPv4`, `winhttp HTTP/2`, `looptest` and
`winsock IPv6` did not finish in 180 s (virtio-net, ping and curl -6 passed).
Both pass under TCG, so they point at kernel races or timing assumptions the
faster guest exposes (the ACPI thread runs without the big kernel lock).
Those jobs set `NOVARUN_ACCEL=tcg` until that is fixed; the tests are not
loosened.  TCG times for reference: boot-test self-tests 228 s, network 63 s.
